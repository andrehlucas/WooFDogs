WE OPERATE ON PURE KINDNESS AND DO NOT CHARGE OR EVER ASK FOR  DONATIONS FOR VISITS.

Unlike other organizations, we will NEVER go to or frequently visit any facility or location based upon their willingness to offer a "donation."

​

Certified Therapy Dog, Inc. is an AKC Recommended, Non-Profit group of caring people dedicated to training and safely volunteering with their Certified Therapy Dog.

​

- ​If you would like tobringjoy, happiness and hope; and promote the physical, mental and emotional well-being that the touch of a dog will provide....

- Our purpose is to help you do just that!

- We are strictly a volunteer organization and NEVER CHARGE FOR VISITS!


​

Whether your goal is to…​

​

- Bring smiles, comfort and joy to people in places that need it most such as: schools, children’s hospitals, rehabilitation centers, Hospice, the juvenile and family courthouse, library reading programs, memory care facilities, assisted living facilities, the VA, counseling sessions, the airport and more.


​

- ALSO obtain all three levels of the AKC Obedience Certifications (CGC, CGCA and CGCU) which are included for free in our therapy dog training curriculum,


​

- Or you just want to enjoy the pride of having an impressively well behaved four-legged friend…


​Certified Therapy Dog, Inc. (CTD) is THE AKC RECOMMENDED organization that will assist you and your furry friend to achieve your goals.
