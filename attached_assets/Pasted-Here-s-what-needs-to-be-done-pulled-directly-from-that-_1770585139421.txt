Here’s what needs to be done, pulled directly from that conversation and organized into an execution checklist (priority order).

1) Lock the preferred domain everywhere (technical SEO)

Goal: only one “version” of the site exists for Google + users.
	•	301 redirect: https://www.woofdogs.com/ → https://woofdogs.com/
	•	Canonicals: every page’s canonical tag points to https://woofdogs.com/...
	•	Sitemap: regenerate/replace sitemap URLs so they’re all https://woofdogs.com (no www, no http)
	•	Internal links cleanup: navigation, footer, breadcrumbs, buttons, in-content links → all use the non-www version
	•	Tracking pixels/embeds: update any scripts (GTM, Meta pixel, call tracking, booking embeds) that reference the www domain

Done when: typing www always lands on non-www, and page source shows canonical = non-www.

⸻

2) Build the Top-5 city pages (local SEO landing pages)

Goal: rank for “dog training + city” and convert.

Create/strengthen these pages (clean URLs):
	•	/dog-training-boca-raton/
	•	/dog-training-wellington/
	•	/dog-training-loxahatchee/
	•	/dog-training-west-palm-beach/
	•	/dog-training-delray-beach/

Each city page should include (same blueprint)
	•	H1: Dog Training in [City]
	•	Service-forward sections (your priority order):
	•	Obedience Training in [City]
	•	Puppy Training in [City]
	•	Service Animal Training in [City]
	•	What makes WooF Dogs different (credentials, philosophy, outcomes)
	•	Common issues you solve (reactivity, leash pulling, jumping, separation anxiety, etc.)
	•	Your process (evaluation → plan → sessions → homework → follow-up)
	•	Proof (testimonials, results, case studies)
	•	Pricing/Packages (at least directional / “starting at” or “call for quote” + what’s included)
	•	NAP / Service Area statement (consistent business info)
	•	Localized FAQ (6–8 questions) tailored to that city
	•	Strong CTA: “Book a free phone consult” / “Schedule an evaluation”

Done when: all 5 pages exist, are indexable, and are linked from the site (next section).

⸻

3) Apply title tags + meta descriptions (templates already defined)

Goal: improve CTR and local relevance.

For each city page:

Title (55–60 chars target):
Dog Training in [City] | WooF Dogs

Meta (150–160 chars target):
Professional dog training in [City]. Obedience + puppy training + service animal training. Serving nearby areas. Call now to schedule a consult.

Done when: titles/metas are implemented on-page (not just in a plugin preview) and visible in GSC once crawled.

⸻

4) Create the internal linking “loop” (indexing + rankings)

Goal: make Google clearly understand the relationship between services ↔ cities.
	•	Homepage: add a “Service Areas” section linking to the 5 city pages
	•	Service pages: add a “Where we work” section linking to the 5 city pages
(at minimum on: Obedience, Puppy, Service Animal pages)
	•	City pages: link back to core service pages (Obedience / Puppy / Service Animal)

Done when: every city page has at least a few internal links pointing to it, and it links back to the service hub pages.

⸻

5) Publishing + QA pass (so you don’t create SEO problems)
	•	Make sure the city pages are unique (no near-duplicate intros) → customize local sections + FAQs
	•	Confirm each city page has:
	•	one H1
	•	clean URL
	•	indexable (no noindex)
	•	fast-loading, mobile-friendly layout
	•	Add them to the HTML sitemap (if you have one) and XML sitemap

⸻

6) Submit + validate in Google Search Console (so Google picks it up faster)

After the above is live:
	•	Submit updated sitemap in GSC
	•	URL Inspection → Request Indexing for each of the 5 city pages
	•	Monitor:
	•	indexing status
	•	impressions/CTR per city page
	•	queries like “dog training boca raton”, “puppy training wellington”, etc.
