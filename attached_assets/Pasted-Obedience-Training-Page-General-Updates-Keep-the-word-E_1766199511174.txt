Advanced Obedience
•
•
•
•
•
•
•
Advanced obedience uses the same core commands as Basic Obedience.
The primary difference is the introduction of the 3Ds:
Duration (longer stays)
Distance (handler moves farther away)
Distraction (controlled environmental challenges)
The Place command is introduced at this level.
Recall is trained using a long line up to 30 feet.

Advanced Off-Leash
•
•
•
•
•
•
•
•
•
Do not reference public off-leash work.
Training takes place in training centers or enclosed areas.
Skills emphasized:
Off-leash heel (continued)
Recall under increased distraction
Distance commands
Place with distractions
Stay with temptation
Distractions may include other dogs, people, wildlife, and moving objects.
