## What Is a Board & Train Program?

A Board & Train program is an intensive dog training solution where your dog lives and trains with our professional trainers for a defined period of time.  
During this stay, dogs participate in multiple daily training sessions focused on obedience commands, behavior improvement, and structured routines.

Our program emphasizes:
- Professional dog training in a controlled environment  
- Consistent, daily training sessions  
- Clear communication and structure  
- Real-life behavior application  

This approach is ideal for dogs that benefit from repetition, structure, and professional oversight.

---

## Is Board & Train Right for Your Dog?

Board & Train may be a great option if:
- Your dog struggles with obedience or impulse control  
- Behavioral issues require consistency and structure  
- You have limited time for daily training sessions  
- Your dog needs a reset in routine and expectations  

This program is **not a one-size-fits-all solution**, which is why every dog starts with a professional behavioral evaluation.

## Board & Train FAQs

### How long does a board and train program last?
Program length varies depending on the dog’s needs and behavioral goals. This is discussed during the evaluation.

Yes. Safety, welfare, and appropriate training intensity are top priorities throughout the program.

Yes. Owner guidance and follow-up recommendations are provided to support long-term success.
