# WooF Dogs — Editorial Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing any new or updated content on the website, blog, or service pages.

---

## 1. Required Sign-Off

- [ ] **Shay or Erica must read and approve every word** before it goes live — including AI-generated drafts, contractor copy, and agency-submitted content.
- [ ] No content is published by a developer, contractor, or AI tool without written approval (Slack message, email, or text) from Shay or Erica.
- [ ] For blog posts: Shay or Erica signs off on the **full article**, not just the headline or summary.
- [ ] For service page copy: Shay or Erica signs off on **every paragraph**, including FAQs, process steps, and structured-data descriptions.

---

## 2. Pre-Publication Line-by-Line Reading

Before hitting publish, one of the approvers must complete this reading pass:

- [ ] Read the article or page from top to bottom — do not skim.
- [ ] Flag any sentence that does not reflect how WooF Dogs actually works.
- [ ] Verify that every factual claim (statistics, pricing, session lengths, program names) is accurate.
- [ ] Confirm that the tone matches the WooF Dogs voice: direct, professional, and owner-focused.
- [ ] For AI-generated content: treat every sentence as unverified until confirmed. AI drafts are starting points, not finished copy.

---

## 3. Service Label Consistency

All visible labels, headings, buttons, card titles, nav items, and structured-data service names must use the approved terms:

| Use this | Not this |
|---|---|
| Evaluation | Behavioral Assessment, Assessment, Behavioral Eval |
| up to 40 minutes | ~40 Min, 40 minutes, forty minutes |
| approximately 40 minutes | ≈40 min, about forty minutes |
| about 40 minutes | (acceptable alternate phrasing) |

- [ ] No heading, button, or card title uses "Behavioral Assessment" as a service label.
- [ ] Any timing reference for the evaluation uses one of the three approved phrases above.
- [ ] CTA link text and inline anchor text use "evaluation" or "Evaluation" as the service label (not "behavioral assessment").
- [ ] Descriptive body prose that was approved in a prior editorial review is preserved exactly — do not rephrase approved paragraphs without a new sign-off.

---

## 4. URL Slug Policy

- [ ] URL slugs are **frozen** — do not change any existing path (e.g., `/behavioral-assessment`, `/obedience`, `/dog-boarding`) without a completed SEO redirect review.
- [ ] Changing a URL requires: (a) 301 redirect in place, (b) all internal links updated, (c) canonical tag updated, (d) sitemap regenerated.
- [ ] New page slugs are reviewed by Shay or Erica before the page is published.

---

## 5. Blog Post Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Frontmatter fields complete: `title`, `description`, `coverImage`, `coverImageAlt`, `date`, `author`, `tags`
- [ ] Meta description is accurate and does not contain unapproved claims
- [ ] All internal links point to valid, existing pages
- [ ] CTA link text uses "Evaluation" (not "Behavioral Assessment")
- [ ] Body prose that explains services accurately reflects current offerings
- [ ] Shay or Erica sign-off confirmed (see Section 1)

---

## 6. Service Page Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Page title and OG title match approved label (e.g., "Evaluation" not "Behavioral Assessment")
- [ ] Timing references use an approved phrase (see Section 3)
- [ ] FAQ answers are accurate and approved
- [ ] Structured data (`@type: Service`) name matches the approved service label
- [ ] Shay or Erica sign-off confirmed (see Section 1)
